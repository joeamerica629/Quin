A spot on Serena Side

Simple food.
Coastal spirit.

Fisherman's Creek is a bar and restaurant on the Serena Side of the creek, in Shanzu — where Mombasa County meets Kilifi County. The kitchen keeps it honest: fish, seafood and grilled favourites, cooked simply and served with a view.

Days roll easy here. Guests come for the creek, stay for the cocktails, and leave talking about the sunset. Themed nights run through the week, with music, a swing over the water and boat rides on offer.

"Pick your catch, choose your side, settle in."
